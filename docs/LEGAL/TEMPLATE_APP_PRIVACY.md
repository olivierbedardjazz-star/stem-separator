# Stem Separator Privacy Policy

Product: Stem Separator
Updated: September 6, 2026
Owner: Olivier Grenier Bedard
Contact: info.mymusicalbrain@gmail.com

Audio processing runs on this Mac. The app does not upload your audio or stems, require an account, charge payments, or include advertising or analytics. Python, Demucs, the model, and required libraries are bundled. Separation does not need an internet connection.

Local settings store the selected theme, responsible-use acceptance version, and a bookmark for the output folder. Source audio is not saved as history. Temporary decoded audio and intermediate stems are written to an app-owned temporary workspace during processing and removed when the job finishes or is cancelled. A crash or force quit may leave temporary files until recovery or macOS cleanup. Completed output files remain in your chosen folder until you remove them.

Operational entries in the macOS unified log contain job lifecycle, elapsed time, processing stage and a system error domain/code. The app does not intentionally log audio, song titles, source paths, output paths, or worker tracebacks. macOS may independently maintain crash reports and system diagnostics under your system settings.

Opening the banner, social links, or contact destination sends your browser to an external service, which receives ordinary browser/network information under its own policy. These links are optional.

Sparkle checks for updates through the public GitHub repository olivierbedardjazz-star/stem-separator. Update checks/downloads contact GitHub and its delivery infrastructure, which receive ordinary connection information such as your IP address and HTTP request metadata. Audio and stems are not included in these requests. Update availability depends on your internet connection and GitHub service availability.

No first-party cloud storage or user profile is created by Stem Separator. To remove local app settings, remove this app's preferences; delete completed stems separately from the output folders you selected.
