# Stem Separator support

Stem Separator is a free, offline Mac app for separating one audio file into four stems or making a karaoke track. It requires an Apple Silicon Mac with macOS 15.1 or later.

For help, email **info.mymusicalbrain@gmail.com**. Include the app version, macOS version, what you clicked, and the exact error shown. Do not send private recordings unless we ask for one and you have permission to share it.

The app's [Mac App Store privacy policy](../../LEGAL/AppStore/APP_STORE_PRIVACY.md) explains local processing and temporary files. Updates for the App Store edition arrive through the Mac App Store.
